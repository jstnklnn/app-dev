# My Favorite Movie 

## Title: *A Silent Voice*  
**Director:** Naoko Yamada  
**Genre:** Drama, Slice of Life, Psychological  
**Studio:** Kyoto Animation  

---

### Short Description
*A Silent Voice* is a touching anime movie about a boy who bullies a deaf girl in elementary school, then seeks redemption years later by trying to reconnect with her. It's a powerful story about guilt, forgiveness, and self-worth.

> "Sometimes we only realize the value of things once we’ve lost them."

---

### Why I Love It
- Emotional storytelling  
- Beautiful animation  
- Deep and relatable characters  
- Teaches empathy and understanding
